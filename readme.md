## Profile

**Mohammad Maizied Hasan Majumder** (`@Maijied`) is a Senior Software Engineer at [Shohoz](https://www.shohoz.com/) in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and the founder of [Lorapok Labs](https://lorapok.tech/). He holds a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST).

At Shohoz he works on backend and full-stack systems for national-scale bus, train, flight, and event booking. In parallel he designs, ships, and maintains 30+ public products across AI agents, developer tools, packages, media, and localization.

Previously: Software Engineer at Infinity Infotech Ltd and Turtle Solutions Limited. 3rd place, IUT Hackathon. Mozilla Firefox localization contributor (2012–2018). Seminar organizer, SUST CSE Society.
